Teacher Onboarding Guide

Use this quick guide to run a classroom pilot with Aforapple.fun.

Recommended 15-minute demo flow

1. Alphabet warm-up Start with the Alphabet Chart on a smart TV or projector and use autoplay for whole-class participation.
2. Phonics practice Move into Phonics to model sound groups and let students call out example words together.
3. Tracing station Use Tracing on a tablet or touchscreen to reinforce English, Hindi, and Telugu character recognition.
4. Whiteboard extension End with the Interactive Whiteboard for teacher-led writing, sound sorting, or drawing prompts.

Best setup for pilots

  • Run the main lesson on a smart TV, projector, or large classroom display.
  • Keep one tablet or laptop nearby for tracing and small-group activities.
  • Use a stable browser with audio enabled before students enter the room.
  • Open the pilot report page at the end of the day to export local usage if needed.

Suggested week-one lesson playlist

  • Day 1: Alphabet Chart -> Tracing -> Whiteboard free play
  • Day 2: Phonics short vowels -> Alphabet review -> Tracing
  • Day 3: Colors or Shapes -> Whiteboard drawing -> Phonics blends
  • Day 4: Numbers or Days -> Tracing language rotation -> Whiteboard recap
  • Day 5: Mixed revision with Alphabet autoplay, Phonics, and selected pilot lessons

What to collect during the pilot

  • Which grades and classrooms used the content most often.
  • Which languages were most useful for teachers and families.
  • Whether smart TV delivery or touch devices worked better in each room.
  • Which activities teachers want customized for their school curriculum.
