Pilot operations

How the 14-day school pilot works

The funnel is designed for high-touch school sales, not a generic consumer signup.

1. Book a short admin demo We confirm grades served, languages needed, classroom count, and device setup before issuing access.
2. Issue a school-specific pilot key The current activation flow stays in place for gated content, but the pilot is framed as school access.
3. Follow up on day 3, 7, and 12 The pilot report page can export device-level usage data for closer follow-up conversations.
4. Convert to annual licensing Move the school to a classroom or multi-classroom plan based on actual usage and curriculum fit.
